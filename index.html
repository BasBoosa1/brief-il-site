About Brief.il

Brief.il is an Israeli daily news brief on technology, AI, business and the economy, written in Hebrew and published on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

What we publish

  • The morning edition: every morning, Sunday to Friday, a carousel of 5 to 8 stories from the last 24 hours.
  • Explainer videos: our own short videos on the day's main story, with Hebrew narration and subtitles.
  • Tech videos: notable product and technology videos from companies and creators, with a Hebrew headline and full credit to the original creator.

Editorial standards

Every story is based on trusted news sources, fact-checked before it is published, and credits its sources. We report what the source says, without speculation or conclusions of our own about people or companies. No politics and no investment advice. Read how we work.

How Brief.il uses TikTok

Brief.il's own publishing tool uses TikTok's official Content Posting API to publish Brief.il's videos to the Brief.il TikTok account, after the account owner authorizes it with Login Kit. It posts only to the authorized account and does not read or store other users' data. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
